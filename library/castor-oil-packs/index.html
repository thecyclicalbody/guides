Castor oil packs

A castor oil pack is a simple evening ritual that many women find calming, and it is traditionally used to support the liver and digestion.

  1. Rub a thin layer of castor oil on the area under your right ribcage, where your liver sits.
  2. Cover it with an old cloth or shirt, because castor oil stains.
  3. Place a hot water bottle on top and rest for 20 to 40 minutes.
  4. Do this a few evenings a week. Do not use it during your period, during pregnancy or when trying to conceive after ovulation.
