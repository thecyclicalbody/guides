Stress, rhythm and sleep

Your evening wind-down

Your body needs time to switch from day to night. An hour of winding down signals to your brain that it can start producing melatonin. Use the example below and adjust it to your own evening.

Time before bedExample
60 minutesDim the lights and switch to warm or orange light
45 minutesTidy up for tomorrow, make a cup of herbal tea; write down anything still on your mind (see Writing it out)
30 minutesWarm shower or bath, skincare, or some gentle stretching
15 minutesRead a paper book, a body scan or a few minutes of belly breathing (see Belly breathing)
BedtimeLights out at your regular time

Write down your own wind-down, try it for a week and adjust what does not fit.
