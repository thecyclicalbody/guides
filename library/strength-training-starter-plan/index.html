Strength training starter plan

This is an example plan for two sessions a week at home or in the gym. Learn the movements with your body weight first, then add weight gradually. Rest at least one day between sessions.

ExerciseWhat it trainsStarting point
Squat (to a chair if needed)Legs and glutes3 sets of 8 to 12
Hip bridgeGlutes and back of the legs3 sets of 10 to 15
Row (with a band or dumbbells)Upper back3 sets of 8 to 12
Push-up (against a wall or bench)Chest, shoulders and arms3 sets of 6 to 12
Lunge or step-upLegs, balance2 to 3 sets of 8 per leg
PlankCore3 times 20 to 30 seconds

When you can do the top of the range with good form and it no longer feels challenging, make it a little heavier or harder. If you have knee or other joint complaints, ask a trainer or physiotherapist to adjust squats, lunges and jumps for you.
