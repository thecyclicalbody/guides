Digestion, liver and lymph

Foods that support your liver

Your liver processes used hormones so they can leave your body through your gut and kidneys. To do that it needs protein, sulfur compounds, B vitamins, antioxidants and a steady flow of bile. Adding the following foods to your meals supports this.

  • Sulfur-rich vegetables: onion, leek, garlic, spring onion, and crucifers such as broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts, kale, cabbage, bok choy and watercress. Aim for one to two portions a day and rotate them through the week. Broccoli sprouts two to three times a week are a rich source.
  • Bitter foods for bile flow: arugula, chicory, endive, radicchio, dandelion greens and artichoke. A handful before or with your meal helps digestion get started.
  • Beetroot, spinach, quinoa and egg yolks for choline and betaine, which the liver uses in its clearance pathways.
  • Colorful fruit: berries, citrus, pomegranate and kiwi.
  • Herbs and spices: turmeric with black pepper, ginger, rosemary, oregano, cumin and coriander.
  • Healthy fats that keep bile moving: olive oil, avocado, olives, walnuts, almonds, flaxseed and chia.
  • Selenium and zinc: one or two Brazil nuts most days, pumpkin seeds, sesame seeds and tahini.
  • Foods for methylation, a key step in clearing estrogen: eggs, fish, meat and dairy for B12 and B2; spinach, asparagus and eggs for folate; bananas, fish and legumes for B6; eggs and cauliflower for choline; pumpkin seeds, chia, cashews and almonds for magnesium.
  • Foods for glutathione, your body's own antioxidant: asparagus, avocado and cucumber.
  • Fiber and a daily bowel movement, so estrogen that your liver has processed leaves your body instead of being reabsorbed (see Fiber and digestion).

A note on crucifers. Crucifers help your liver steer estrogen into its most protective breakdown pathway. If your estrogen is already low, for example when your cycle is very long or absent, we keep them to a normal portion rather than large amounts.

Alcohol puts extra demand on the same pathways your liver uses to clear hormones, so reducing it is one of the most effective changes you can make.
