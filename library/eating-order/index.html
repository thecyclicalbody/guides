Meals and blood sugar

Eating order

The order in which you eat the parts of your meal changes how fast sugar enters your blood. When you start with vegetables and then protein and fat, your stomach empties more slowly and the carbohydrates that follow cause a smaller rise in blood sugar. Studies in people with and without diabetes found clearly lower blood sugar peaks after a meal eaten in this order, compared to the same meal with the carbohydrates first.

  1. Start with the vegetables.
  2. Then eat the protein and fat.
  3. Finish with the carbohydrates, and eat fruit as dessert rather than on its own.

It is nice to eat a little bit of everything when you eat, and you do not have to be strict about it. Just make sure you start with a few bites of vegetables or a small salad, and not with all the carbs.
