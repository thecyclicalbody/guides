Stress, rhythm and sleep

Writing it out

Writing helps you get thoughts out of your head, so they take up less space. Choose the form that suits you.

Morning pages. Write about three pages by hand in the morning, without stopping and without rereading. Whatever comes up is fine, including "I don't know what to write".

Worry time. Choose a fixed 10 minutes in the day, not just before bed. Write down everything you are worrying about. When a worry comes up at another moment, tell yourself you will look at it during worry time.

Starter questions if you get stuck

  • What is taking up most space in my head today?
  • What am I worried about, and what part of it can I actually influence?
  • What do I need today, and from whom?
  • What went well today, however small?
