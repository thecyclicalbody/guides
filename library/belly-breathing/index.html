Stress, rhythm and sleep

Belly breathing

Slow breathing into your belly activates the part of your nervous system that is responsible for rest and digestion. Do this twice a day for 3 to 5 minutes, for example after waking and before going to sleep.

  1. Sit or lie down comfortably and place one hand on your chest and one on your belly.
  2. Breathe in slowly through your nose for about 4 counts, and let your belly rise under your hand while your chest stays fairly still.
  3. Breathe out slowly through your nose or slightly open mouth for about 6 counts, and feel your belly sink.
  4. Repeat for 3 to 5 minutes. If your mind wanders, gently bring your attention back to your hand on your belly.

If focusing on your breath makes you restless or anxious, which happens to some people, skip this exercise and use the Body check-in for stress or a short walk instead.
