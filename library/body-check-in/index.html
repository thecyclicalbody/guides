Body check-in for stress

Stress often shows up in your body before you notice it in your thoughts. This check-in takes a minute and helps you recognize your own stress signals.

  1. Pause and notice your breathing: is it fast, shallow, or held?
  2. Scan your body from head to toes. Where do you feel tension? Many people feel it in the jaw, shoulders, stomach or chest.
  3. Name what you feel in a few words, for example "tight chest" or "buzzing in my legs". Naming it often already makes it a little smaller.
  4. Ask yourself what would help right now: sitting still, a few slow breaths, or moving. Some people calm down best by shaking out their arms and legs, dancing to one song or going for a short walk.

Note what you noticed for a week. Over time you will learn your own early warning signs.
