Meals and blood sugar

Hunger awareness

This exercise helps you notice the difference between physical hunger and eating for other reasons, such as stress, boredom or habit. Each check-in takes 15 to 30 seconds. There are no right or wrong answers; you are simply collecting information about your body.

MomentQuestions to ask yourself
Before eatingDo I feel physical hunger, and where? Am I hungry for a snack or a full meal? How is my energy right now?
During eatingAm I eating slowly, at a normal pace or fast? Am I chewing properly? What tastes is my body asking for: hearty, salty, sweet?
Toward the endHow do I know I am getting full, and which sensations tell me? Is there still room, or is it more of a craving for something sweet?
1 to 2 hours afterHow has my energy and focus been since eating? Am I hungry again already? Did this hunger feel like a quiet signal or an emergency?

After a week, look back: which meals kept you satisfied the longest, and do you notice any patterns in when or why you eat?
