Meals and blood sugar

Building your plate

A balanced plate keeps your blood sugar steady, keeps you full for longer and gives your body the building blocks it needs to make hormones.

  • Half of your plate: non-starchy vegetables, at least two cups or two big handfuls, cooked or raw. Think of leafy greens, broccoli, zucchini, carrots, peppers, green beans or cabbage.
  • A quarter of your plate: protein, that is roughly 85 to 140 g of fish, meat, tofu or tempeh, or 2 to 3 eggs. This is deliberately generous, because your body needs protein to build hormones.
  • A quarter of your plate: carbohydrates, about ½ to 1 cup cooked. Choose whole sources such as potatoes, sweet potato, rice, quinoa, buckwheat, oats or legumes.
  • Fat on top: a tablespoon of olive oil, a knob of butter or ghee, half an avocado, or a handful of nuts or seeds. Fat helps you absorb vitamins A, D, E and K and keeps you satisfied.
