Living with your cycle

Your energy, focus and need for rest change across your cycle, because your hormone levels change. When you plan with these changes rather than against them, many women find their weeks feel calmer and more productive. This is not a strict schedule, but a way to notice your own pattern and use it.

PhaseRoughlyWhat many women noticeIdeas for planning
MenstrualDays 1 to 5Lower energy, more need for rest and reflectionKeep the agenda light, plan evaluation and reflection, gentle movement
FollicularDays 6 to 13Rising energy and curiosityStart new projects, learn and brainstorm, more intense training
OvulatoryDays 14 to 17Peak energy, more social and verbalPresentations, conversations, meetings and social plans
LutealDays 18 to 28Energy turns inward, more focus on detail, later more need for restFinish tasks, admin and organizing, lighter training toward your period

The days are based on a 28-day cycle; if your cycle is shorter or longer, your phases shift with it. Tracking your cycle shows when your phases actually fall.
