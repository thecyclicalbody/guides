Your cycle

Your cycle story

The way you think about your cycle is shaped by what you learned growing up, at school and from the people around you. These questions help you look at that story. Take your time, and answer only the ones that speak to you.

Looking back

  • What do you remember about your first period, and how did the people around you react?
  • What did you learn about periods at home and at school?

Right now

  • How do you feel about your body during your period and in the rest of your cycle?
  • Which words do you use for your period, and how do they feel?
  • How much room do you give yourself to rest when you need it?

Looking ahead

  • How would you like to feel about your cycle?
  • What would you like to do differently, for yourself or for others around you?
