Digestion, liver and lymph

Hydration and minerals

Your body needs water and minerals for digestion, energy and a regular bowel movement. Aim for 1.8 to 2 liters a day, including herbal tea and soup, and more on warm days or when you sweat.

  • Add a pinch of good-quality salt to your water, or take one electrolyte sachet a day, especially if you drink filtered water or are under stress.
  • Good choices are water, mineral water and herbal teas such as ginger, nettle, peppermint, chamomile or rooibos.
  • Drink mostly between meals rather than large amounts during meals.
